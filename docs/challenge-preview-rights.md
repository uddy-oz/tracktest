# Music Preview Rights: Open Release Gate

The Shareable Challenges migration does not grant rights to play or replay
music. A preview URL being reachable, or the app streaming it without storing
the audio file, is not evidence of permission to use it in gameplay.

[Apple's iTunes Search API documentation](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/documentation/AudioVideo/Conceptual/iTuneSearchAPI/index.html)
describes previews as promotional content and limits entertainment use
independent of that promotion. StanZer has not established authorization for
playing those clips in a shareable music quiz. This concern also applies to the
existing quiz use and should be reviewed separately from the database rollout.

Do not treat release approval as music authorization. Resolve this gate through
appropriate written permission or a provider or catalog licensed for the
intended quiz use. Until then, use original generated or otherwise authorized
audio for private QA. Do not download, cache, rehost, or redistribute Apple's
audio to work around the restriction.
